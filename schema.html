No "Architect Now, Build Later" tables this phase. Every V2 item (academy-wide scheduling, Center Manager/Admin screens, multi-center rollout, multiple report templates) reuses the tables above via role and center_id — nothing speculative was added. Payments/CRM are permanently out of KOMS's scope and stay on OClass; they were never modeled here.
